Shield Policy

It is understandable to forget a shield now and again — we are all human.

When we face an opponent like ASG, forgetting to shield costs both you and the people who support you. Losses in resources and healing time can be recouped — duel points cannot. That matters most when our duel is close.

Starting Next Kill Event

If you fail to shield and are about to be attacked

Leadership will remove you from the alliance — temporarily.

Doing this denies the opposition 4.5× the points they would have taken if you had stayed in the alliance.

Getting back in

When you log back in, apply and leadership will accept your request.

Also Starting Next Kill Event

Unshielded & offline 1 hour or more

Your rank will be demoted until the start of the next Kill Event.

Questions? Ask leadership before the next Kill Event starts.
